Special Considerations

Delays may occasionally occur due to circumstances beyond our control, including:

  • Natural calamities, power outages, or extreme weather conditions.
  • Peak holiday seasons — particularly August, September, and October — including Diwali and Durga Puja.
  • Hartals, public holidays, Saturdays, and Sundays — no shipments or deliveries occur on these days.
